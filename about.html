About me

I’m interested in the moment someone decides to keep watching.

I’m Praise, a freelance short-form video editor helping creators make their best ideas clearer, sharper, and more themselves. I care about the invisible work: the pause before a reveal, the sound that makes a cut feel right, the caption that arrives exactly on time.

My editing journey started with curiosity and a lot of late nights. It grew into a practice built on instinct, structure, and a genuine love for the craft. Today I work closely with creators who want a thoughtful partner, not just someone to move clips around.
